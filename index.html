M.E.R.LIN: The Vision of Accessibility

With M.E.R.LIN, people with motor limitations discover the freedom of using computers independently and intuitively. The software, always ready to assist, transforms digital tasks into accessible and inclusive experiences, making technology more accessible and available to everyone !

How is This Possible?

M.E.R.LIN in Action

With simple facial and eye movements, the software allows you to navigate, click, and type intuitively. Each gesture is interpreted accurately, demonstrating how technology can make digital tasks more accessible, fun, and independent for people with motor limitations.
